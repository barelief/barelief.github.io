Bartosz Połoński

About

Bartosz Połoński was born in Vilnius, studied in Poland and has worked in the United States.

His debut novel, Robczik. Powieść wileńska (2023), follows a group of Polish-speaking high-school students in Vilnius in the early 21st century. Written in the city's own street language, a mix of Polish, Lithuanian, youth slang, Russian and English, it shows contemporary Vilnius and its young people without nostalgic myths. The novel was published in Polish by Części Proste and in Lithuanian, as Robčikas: vilenskas romanas, by Slinktys. The Lithuanian edition is also available as an audiobook, and he adapted it himself for the stage of the Polish Theatre in Vilnius.

Neovileika, his new collection of short stories written in Lithuanian as Bartas Polonskis, is about a past that keeps coming back: through family secrets, identity crises and the technology we live with.
